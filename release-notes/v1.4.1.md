> [!TIP]
> **Installing this update keeps everything.** Download `LMusic-1.4.1.apk` below on your phone, open it and tap **Update**. Your library, playlists, listening stats and settings stay as they are.

LMusic 1.4.1 brings YouTube Music and runs on Android 13 and 14.

### New
- **YouTube Music:** search, browse and play YouTube Music, with radio, artist and album pages, search suggestions and lyrics. Download songs as tagged `.opus` files (saved in `Music/LMusic/YouTube Music`), watch music videos with picture-in-picture, and pick a streaming and download quality in Settings › Playback › YouTube Music. Sign in to see your YouTube Music library and sync likes. Your phone's songs never go online.
- **Android 13 and 14:** LMusic now installs on Android 13 and newer (it needed Android 15 before).
- **Contributors** on the About screen. Thanks to Shaan Shoukath for YouTube Music.

### Fixed
- Searching YouTube Music for some artists closed the app. The same artist or song is no longer shown twice in a list.

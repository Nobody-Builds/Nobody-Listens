Nobody Listens 1.5.1 is a bug-fix update for watch CPU use during playback.

### Fixed
- The watch player no longer rebuilds its whole screen whenever the playback position changes.
- Position updates no longer rebuild the entire queue or recheck artwork every second.
- Background and ambient mode cancel UI polling immediately; progress catches up when the player returns.

### Performance
- The play star and playing-song bars stay still during playback. Brief play/pause transitions remain.
- The progress ring updates once per second and reuses its drawing paths.
- Pager pages and the full-screen artwork overlay load when needed.

YouTube Music, JioSaavn, offline songs and playlists, search, handoff, queue controls and volume remain available.
Audio quality, streaming buffers, Wi-Fi handling and the Nobody Builds Daylight design are unchanged.

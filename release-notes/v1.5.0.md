> [!TIP]
> **Nobody Listens installs as its own app** (`com.nobodybuilds.listens`), next to LMusic. To bring your library,
> playlists and stats over, back up in LMusic (Settings › Backup & Restore) and restore the file here.

Nobody Listens 1.5.0 is the first release of this fork of [LMusic](https://github.com/twilighty-abhi/lmusic-player)
by Abhiram NJ: the Nobody Builds brand and "Daylight" design on Material 3, and a Galaxy Watch app that works
without the phone.

### New
- **Nobody Listens:** new name, logo and app icon, the Nobody Builds "Daylight" colours and type on Material 3
  (Material You still one tap away in Settings › Appearance › App colours), and a party page favicon that follows
  light and dark mode. About lists the developers, Shaan Shoukath and Abhiram NJ, with links to both projects.
- **JioSaavn:** a second streaming service next to YouTube Music, with the same screens: feed, library, search
  (a JioSaavn chip on Search), album, playlist and artist pages, radio, lyrics, up to 320 kbps, and tagged `.m4a`
  downloads to `Music/Nobody Listens/JioSaavn`. YouTube Music and JioSaavn playlists and albums can be imported as
  your own playlists.
- **Galaxy Watch, standalone:**
  - Search the watch's songs and the phone's library; tap a chip to search YouTube Music or JioSaavn too.
  - Stream YouTube Music and JioSaavn straight from the watch over its own Wi-Fi or LTE, with the cover behind the
    player.
  - Save a whole phone playlist to the watch and play it with no phone (Library › Watch playlists).
  - "Save to watch" now works from the watch's own song lists.
  - A new look: Daylight colours, Gilroy titles, layouts for round and square watches, and the bezel or crown
    handled by the app (one item per bezel click on Galaxy watches, volume on the player).
- **YouTube search suggestions** can be turned on in Settings › Playback › YouTube Music (off by default).
- **Every APK type** on each release: arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a, x86_64, universal and the watch.

### Fixed
- Notifications and song details for YouTube songs no longer show play, view or like counts as the artist or album.
- Smoother scrolling in Settings, Stats, Equalizer and other screens with a collapsing title, and smoother Library
  tab changes.
- The watch's song database upgrades cleanly; its upgrade steps were never registered before.
- Searching YouTube Music for some artists closed the app (from LMusic 1.4.1).
